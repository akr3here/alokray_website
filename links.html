Links

Things on the internet I keep returning to.

  • Paul Graham — Essays
    paulgraham.com

    Where most of my thinking about writing and ideas started. Start with "How to Think for Yourself" or "What You'll Wish You'd Known."

  • Derek Sivers — Notes
    sive.rs

    Short, direct, honest. Every post makes you think. The man also invented the /now page, which I borrowed.

  • Robin Sloan
    robinsloan.com

    A writer who thinks about the internet in interesting ways. His newsletter is one of the few I actually read.

  • Naval Ravikant
    nav.al

    Dense ideas about wealth, happiness, and how to live. Some of it is obvious in retrospect, but he says it more clearly than anyone else.

  • Goodreads
    goodreads.com

    Flawed but useful for tracking books and finding what to read next.
