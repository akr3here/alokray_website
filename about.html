About

Who I am and how I think.

Hi, I'm Alok. I live in India and I write about things that interest me — technology, ideas, the way people work and think.

I started this site because I wanted a place to think out loud. Not a newsletter, not a brand — just a place to put words and see what comes of it. A small, quiet corner of the internet that's mine.

I believe writing is a tool for thinking, not just communicating. The best essays are ones where the author is figuring something out in real time, and takes you along for the ride.

This site is also my attempt at documentation — of life, of reading, of ideas that passed through. It will grow slowly and change over time. That's the point.

If something here resonates, I'd love to hear from you.
